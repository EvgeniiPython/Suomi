# Lesson Flow

This is the standard lesson sequence for Finnish learning sessions.

## 1. Due-chunk recall

- Read the current date.
- Load all active chunks whose `Next Review` is today or earlier.
- Give the learner the Russian meaning only.
- Learner recalls the Finnish chunk without seeing the answer.
- Do not introduce new material before the due set has been checked.

## 2. First full recall

- Learner gives the professional-process story or target dialogue from memory.
- Teacher does not interrupt during the recall.
- Learner signals completion with: "Я закончил, говори."

## 3. Error correction

- Identify only the most useful errors and unstable chunks.
- Correct grammar, word choice, word order and chunk boundaries.
- Prefer chunk-level correction over isolated vocabulary explanations.

## 4. Second recall

- Learner immediately reproduces the corrected chunk(s) again.
- Then repeat the whole story or relevant section without interruption.
- This is the main correction-to-automaticity step.

## 5. New material

- Add only 1–2 new chunks when the due review set is complete.
- New chunks must be useful in the learner's professional context.
- Teach the chunk as a ready-to-use unit, not as disconnected words.

## 6. Context integration

- Insert the new chunk into the existing professional story.
- Vary the context or tense when useful.
- Use recall first; explanation comes after an attempt.

## 7. Final recall

- Learner gives one clean uninterrupted version of the story.
- Teacher waits for the completion signal before feedback.

## 8. Session close

Record:
- chunks successfully recalled;
- chunks recalled only after correction;
- recurring errors;
- new chunks introduced or activated;
- next review dates;
- next lesson focus;
- concise performance metrics.

## Retention protocol

For newly activated chunks, schedule reviews at Day 1, Day 3, Day 7, Day 14 and Day 30. If a due recall needs substantial correction, use the next short interval rather than falsely marking the chunk as automatic.

## Method principle

The lesson is built around active recall, correction, retry and contextual reuse. The goal is automatic production of professional Finnish chunks, not passive recognition.
