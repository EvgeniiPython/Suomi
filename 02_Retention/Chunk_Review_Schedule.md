# Chunk Review Schedule

This table is checked at the start of each lesson. The daily lesson should surface entries whose **Next Review** date is today or earlier.

## Rule
For every newly activated chunk, schedule reviews at:
- Day 1
- Day 3
- Day 7
- Day 14
- Day 30

A review is not just rereading: use recall first, then check/correct/retry, then vary the chunk and use it in context.

When a due recall requires correction, the next review is scheduled conservatively at the next short interval rather than pretending the chunk was fully automatic.

## Daily selection rule
At the beginning of a lesson:
1. Read today's date.
2. Select all rows with **Next Review <= today** and status Active.
3. Recall those chunks without hints.
4. After recall, update the row's review status and calculate the next interval according to the retention protocol.
5. Do not add new chunks until the due review set has been checked.
